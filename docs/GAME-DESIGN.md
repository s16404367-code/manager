# Game design notes
- **Loop:** Information → Uncertainty → Decision → Consequence → Adaptation.
- **Asymmetry:** 12 car attributes. Each of the 16 circuits weights cornering by speed band, straight-line, mechanical grip and braking differently, plus cooling. No single car or setup is optimal everywhere.
- **Trade-offs:** every upgrade shows cost, time, a gain range, failure risk and side-effects. Setups have side-effects, and so do driving and ERS modes. Crunch speeds development but builds fatigue. Next-year focus costs current-season progress.
- **Information quality:** difficulty, the Strategy department and the Analysis Centre scale forecast accuracy. Values are shown as ranges or words; upgrade gains stay hidden until measured.
- **Compressed races:** shorter races scale tyre wear, fuel burn and reliability by full distance ÷ race distance, so pit strategy still matters.
- **Difficulty:** changes information quality, costs, AI decision quality, event frequency and board patience. It never simply makes the AI faster.
